# Licences of the Business starter site

## Photographs

15 images under `media/files/` are photographs taken from Wikimedia Commons, where each is recorded as CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). They were chosen for the section they appear in, then cropped and compressed by `tools/sites/photos.mjs`. The photographer, the Commons page and the date the licence was read are listed for every file in [`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md), which travels with the design, and, for programs, in `photos.json`.

CC0 covers copyright only. These photographs show a person who can be recognised (`team-1.webp`, `team-2.webp`, `team-3.webp`, `team-4.webp`). In the starter site they stand for invented people; anyone who builds a real site from this design should replace them with pictures of their own people. Halden Advisory, its clients, figures and quotes are invented.

## Fonts

The design uses Hanken Grotesk, which is part of the Open Frame theme (see the theme's `readme.txt` for its licence); no font file is packaged with the design.
