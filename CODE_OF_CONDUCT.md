# Code of Conduct

This project wants to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or propose a change without being made to feel unwelcome.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and patient. People here differ in experience, language and background.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intentions, and say plainly when something is not clear to you.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and give reasons when you disagree.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or discriminating remarks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting

If someone behaves against this code, tell the maintainer, [Mostafij Emon](https://github.com/Mostafijemon24), through GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or exclude a person from the project.
